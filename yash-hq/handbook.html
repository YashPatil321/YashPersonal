HANDBOOK

Every hand move and every line you can say. Gestures need the monitor camera on. Everything else works by voice: start with "hey jarvis", or show a peace sign.

The one ruleRaise your hand to about shoulder height, toward the camera, hold it still for half a second, then make the gesture and keep it about a second. Hands at desk height, hands passing by, fidgeting and waving at someone else are ignored.

Touch mode say "touch mode"

Your hand in the air becomes the mouse for the whole screen. The cursor follows your knuckles, so pinching doesn't make it jump.

Move your handMoves the cursor.
PinchClick. Pinch twice fast to double click.
Pinch and hold, moveDrag: select text, move files, sliders.
Thumb to middle fingerRight click.
FistGrab the window under the cursor and carry it. Open your hand to drop.
Fist, then throwSnaps the window to that half of the screen.
Two fingers, moveScroll up and down.
Both hands pinchingPull apart to zoom in, push together to zoom out.
Open palm, 2 secondsLeaves touch mode.
